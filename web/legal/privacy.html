Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

1. Summary

Central de Cortes is a private tool used only by the owner of the Ideias em Comum channel to publish clips to their own accounts. It does not collect data from the public, does not have user sign-up, does not show ads and does not sell or share data with anyone.

2. Data we access from TikTok

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar of the connected account, used only to show which account is connected and where a clip will be posted.
  • Creator posting settings: the privacy options, interaction settings and maximum video length returned by TikTok for that account, used only to build the posting screen.
  • Posting (video.upload, video.publish): used only to upload the clips the owner approved, with the settings the owner chose, to the owner's own account, and to check the status of those posts.

We do not read followers, messages, comments, likes or any other account data.

3. Data we access from YouTube

Through the YouTube Data API we read the name of the connected channel and upload the clips the owner approved. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy.

4. How data is stored

  • Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own server, are never shown in the browser and are used only to perform the actions above.
  • Videos, clips and transcriptions are processed on the owner's own computers. Clip files are deleted from the server a few days after being published.
  • No data is sent to third parties other than the platforms the clips are published to and the AI provider used to select clip segments from the transcription of the owner's own videos.

5. Your control

The owner can disconnect TikTok or YouTube at any time in the app ("Disconnect"), which deletes the stored tokens, or revoke access in the platform's own settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). To request deletion of any remaining data, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than the channel owner.

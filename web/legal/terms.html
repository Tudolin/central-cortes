1. What this service is

Central de Cortes ("the app") is a private, self-hosted tool operated by the Ideias em Comum channel ("we"). It turns the channel's own long-form videos into short vertical clips and, after explicit approval by the account owner, publishes them to the channel's own accounts on YouTube and TikTok.

2. Who may use it

The app is not offered to the public. Only the owner of the Ideias em Comum channel and people they authorize may use it, and only to publish to accounts they own or are authorized to manage. There is no sign-up and no paid plan.

3. Connected accounts

To publish, the account owner connects their YouTube and/or TikTok account through the official login of each platform (OAuth). The owner can disconnect an account at any time in the app or in the platform's own settings. The app acts only on the account that authorized it and only within the permissions granted.

4. Content and consent

  • The app only processes videos the account owner has the right to publish.
  • Nothing is posted without the owner's explicit approval of each clip. For TikTok, the owner chooses the privacy level, interaction settings (comments, Duet, Stitch) and commercial content disclosure for every post before approving it.
  • By publishing to TikTok through the app, the owner agrees to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and, when applicable, the Branded Content Policy.
  • The owner remains responsible for the content they publish and for complying with the terms of YouTube and TikTok.

5. Third-party platforms

Publishing depends on the YouTube Data API and the TikTok Content Posting API. Use of those platforms is governed by their own terms: YouTube Terms of Service and TikTok Terms of Service.

6. Availability and liability

The app is provided "as is", without warranties. We are not liable for failures of third-party platforms, rejected uploads or content removed by those platforms.

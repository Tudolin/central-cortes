Short clips from our own long-form videos

Central de Cortes is a private, self-hosted tool used by the Ideias em Comum channel to turn its own podcasts and debates into short vertical clips with captions, review each clip, and publish the approved ones to the channel's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and it only ever posts to accounts owned by the creator who authorized it.

1. ClipThe creator's own long video is transcribed and split into short vertical clips with captions.
2. ReviewThe creator watches each clip, edits it if needed and chooses the TikTok privacy, interaction and disclosure settings.
3. Consent & postOnly after the creator explicitly approves, the clip is posted to the creator's own account at the chosen time.
